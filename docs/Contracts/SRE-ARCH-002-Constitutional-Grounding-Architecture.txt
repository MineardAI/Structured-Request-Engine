# SRE-ARCH-002
# Constitutional Grounding Architecture

**Document ID:** SRE-ARCH-002  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Candidate Draft  
**Classification:** Architectural Specification  
**Authority:** Structured Request Engine Constitutional Architecture

---

# 1. Purpose

This Architectural Specification defines the constitutional architecture governing **Grounding** within the Structured Request Engine (SRE).

Grounding establishes the constitutional basis upon which represented semantic artifacts are supported while preserving strict separation between:

- representation,
- grounding,
- provenance,
- evaluation,
- truth,
- authorization.

This specification defines reusable constitutional architecture.

It does **not** define contract-specific behavior.

Contracts implementing grounding SHALL conform to this specification.

Initially these include:

- SRE-CONTRACT-007 — Evidence Representation
- SRE-CONTRACT-008 — Provenance Representation

---

# 2. Constitutional Definition

## Grounding

**Grounding** is the constitutional representation of support relationships between represented evidence and represented constitutional artifacts.

Grounding records that constitutional support has been represented.

Grounding SHALL NOT determine:

- adequacy,
- correctness,
- relevance,
- sufficiency,
- confidence,
- truth,
- authorization.

Grounding is representational.

Grounding is not evaluative.

---

# 3. Constitutional Objectives

This architecture SHALL establish a reusable constitutional framework for:

- representing evidence;
- representing support relationships;
- preserving immutable constitutional support;
- enabling deterministic replay;
- enabling auditability;
- enabling provenance;
- enabling constitutional lineage.

This architecture SHALL NOT:

- determine correctness;
- determine truth;
- determine semantic meaning;
- determine relevance;
- determine evidence sufficiency;
- authorize actions;
- resolve conflicts;
- perform policy evaluation.

# 5. Constitutional Principles

## Principle 1 — Representation Before Judgment

Grounding SHALL represent constitutional support.

Grounding SHALL defer all evaluative decisions.

---

## Principle 2 — Evidence Independence

Evidence SHALL remain constitutionally independent from the artifacts it supports.

Semantic artifacts SHALL NOT own evidence.

Evidence MAY support multiple constitutional artifacts.

---

## Principle 3 — Support Independence

Support SHALL exist only through explicit Grounding Assertions.

Support SHALL NOT be inferred solely from references.

---

## Principle 4 — Immutable Constitutional Grounding

Grounding SHALL be immutable.

Additional constitutional support SHALL create new constitutional objects.

Previously published constitutional objects SHALL NOT be modified.

---

## Principle 5 — Bounded Authority

Grounding SHALL establish constitutional support.

Grounding SHALL NOT establish:

- correctness;
- truth;
- confidence;
- preference;
- relevance;
- sufficiency;
- authority.

---

## Principle 6 — Representation Does Not Imply Evaluation

Representation SHALL NEVER imply:

- evaluation;
- correctness;
- truth;
- authorization.

# 6. Constitutional Layering Model

The Constitutional Grounding Architecture operates within the following complementary constitutional layering model.

## Constitutional Representation Layer

Answers:

> What constitutional artifacts have been represented?

Produces semantic constitutional objects.

Examples include:

- Objectives
- Constraints
- Capability Requirements
- Ambiguity
- Assumptions

---

## Constitutional Grounding Layer

Answers:

> Why does a represented constitutional artifact exist?

Produces constitutional support.

Examples include:

- Evidence Representation
- Grounding Assertions
- Grounding Sets

---

## Constitutional Processing Layer

Answers:

> What deterministic constitutional processing occurs after grounding?

Produces deterministic constitutional transformations.

Examples include:

- Reconciliation
- Canonicalization
- Validation

These layers describe constitutional responsibilities.

They SHALL NOT be interpreted as mandatory runtime execution phases.

# 7. Constitutional Object Model

The Constitutional Grounding Architecture defines the following constitutional objects.

---

## 7.1 Evidence Representation

Evidence Representation records constitutional supporting material.

It answers:

> What supporting material has been represented?

Evidence Representation SHALL NOT determine:

- truth;
- correctness;
- relevance;
- sufficiency.

---

## 7.2 Grounding Assertion

Grounding Assertion records one constitutional support relationship.

It answers:

> What constitutional artifact is supported?

Grounding Assertions SHALL:

- possess immutable identity;
- remain independently versionable;
- remain independently auditable;
- participate in provenance.

Grounding Assertions SHALL NOT own:

- evidence;
- semantic artifacts.

Grounding Assertions own only constitutional support relationships.

Grounding Assertions SHALL be issued only by an authorized Grounding Representation Operation operating under an applicable Grounding Representation Profile.

---

## 7.3 Grounding Representation Profile

Grounding Representation Profiles govern:

- representation structure;
- supported relationship forms;
- permitted attachment mechanisms;
- structural validity.

Profiles SHALL NOT perform:

- assessment;
- ranking;
- preference;
- relevance determination;
- sufficiency evaluation.

---

## 7.4 Grounding Set

Grounding Set represents the immutable publication produced by one Grounding Representation Operation.

Grounding Sets SHALL:

- possess immutable identity;
- represent one publication event;
- support deterministic replay;
- support versioned publication.

Grounding Sets SHALL NOT accumulate assertions over time.

New publication SHALL produce a new Grounding Set.

---

## 7.5 Grounding Failure Record

Grounding Failure Record represents the immutable constitutional artifact produced when a Grounding Representation Operation cannot successfully construct a Grounding Set while preserving constitutional invariants.

Failure Records SHALL:

- preserve auditability;
- preserve replayability;
- preserve architectural accountability.

Failure Records SHALL NOT authorize alternative grounding behavior.

---

# 8. Canonical Grounding Pattern

Grounding SHALL follow the Canonical Grounding Pattern.

```text
Grounding Authority
        ↓
Grounding Representation Operation
        ↓
Grounding Representation Profile
        ↓
Evidence Representation
        ↓
Grounding Assertion
        ↓
Grounding Set
        ↓
Grounding Failure Record
        ↓
Deferred Constitutional Decisions
```

This pattern defines constitutional relationships.

It SHALL NOT prescribe implementation order.

---

# 9. Identity Model

Grounding defines independent constitutional identities.

## Evidence Identity

Identifies represented evidence.

Evidence identity SHALL remain stable regardless of additional support relationships.

---

## Grounding Assertion Identity

Identifies one immutable constitutional support relationship.

Each Grounding Assertion SHALL possess a unique identity.

Grounding Assertions SHALL NOT be modified after publication.

---

## Grounding Set Identity

Identifies one immutable publication produced by one Grounding Representation Operation.

Additional support SHALL produce new Grounding Sets.

Previously published Grounding Sets SHALL remain immutable.

---

# 10. Constitutional Relationships

Grounding relationships SHALL remain explicit constitutional objects.

Supported relationship forms MAY include:

- Supports
- Partially Supports
- References
- Derived From
- Aggregates
- Refines
- Supersedes

Relationship semantics remain representational only.

---

Grounding SHALL NOT establish:

Representation SHALL NEVER imply evaluation.

# 14. Future Evolution (Informative)

This section is informative and non-normative.

A recurring constitutional lifecycle pattern has emerged across multiple architectural domains:

```text
Authority
        ↓
Constitutional Operation
        ↓
Immutable Constitutional Object
        ↓
Published Set
        ↓
Deferred Constitutional Decisions
```

The pattern appears consistently across representation and grounding.

However, it has not yet been established as a universal constitutional architecture.

If future architectural evidence confirms that this lifecycle is reusable across the Structured Request Engine, it SHOULD be extracted into a dedicated architectural specification (provisionally **SRE-ARCH-003 — Constitutional Lifecycle Pattern**).

Until such confirmation exists, this specification limits itself to constitutional grounding.
