section 6 Source Admission Lifestyle

6. Source Admission Lifecycle

Specification level: Required Observable Behavior

Contract 001 defines the deterministic lifecycle by which externally supplied submissions become constitutionally admitted source artifacts.

This lifecycle governs only admission.

It SHALL NOT assign semantic meaning, construct request elements, or authorize downstream behavior.

6.1 Intake Initiation

SRE-001-INTAKE-001

Source admission SHALL begin when an externally supplied submission is presented to the Source Admission Boundary.

An intake operation SHALL be initiated for each submission event.

Every intake operation SHALL possess exactly one immutable submission identity.

Submission identities SHALL remain stable throughout the lifetime of the intake operation.

Constitutional Principle

Submission identity identifies the admission event.

It does not identify:

admitted sources;
canonical requests;
semantic meaning;
downstream authority.
6.2 Submission Enumeration

SRE-001-INTAKE-002

Before admission evaluation begins, the Source Admission Boundary SHALL enumerate all submitted source components.

Enumeration SHALL identify:

directly supplied sources;
externally referenced sources;
application-supplied components;
implementation-defined constitutional inputs.

Enumeration SHALL preserve the externally observable structure of the submission.

Enumeration SHALL NOT infer missing components.

Enumeration Rule

The order in which components are received SHALL NOT alter constitutional meaning.

Deterministic ordering requirements belong to later contracts.

6.3 Admission Evaluation

SRE-001-INTAKE-003

Every enumerated component SHALL undergo admission evaluation.

Admission evaluation SHALL determine only whether the component satisfies constitutional intake requirements.

Admission evaluation MAY consider:

encoding support;
structural admissibility;
implementation limits;
required submission fields;
reference admissibility.

Admission evaluation SHALL NOT consider:

semantic correctness;
inferred intent;
request quality;
objective completeness;
policy compliance.
Admissibility

A component SHALL receive one admission evaluation result:

Admissible

Rejected

Unavailable

Unsupported

Incomplete

Malformed

The resulting constitutional source admission state SHALL remain one of the states defined by `SRE-001-SOURCE-002`, including `Pending`, `Received`, `Accepted`, `Rejected`, `Unavailable`, `Malformed`, `Unsupported`, `Incomplete`, or `Redacted`.

For terminal Composite Admission Policy mapping, an `Admissible` evaluation result corresponds to the `Accepted` source admission state unless the applicable policy expressly preserves another contract-authorized state.

No semantic classification SHALL occur during admission evaluation.

6.3.1 Composite Admission Policy Resolution

SRE-001-POLICY-001

The Source Admission Boundary SHALL resolve exactly one Composite Admission Policy before terminal operation commitment.

When no expressly authorized versioned intake profile is supplied, the boundary SHALL use the Contract 001 default Composite Admission Policy defined in Section 5.6.2.

SRE-001-POLICY-002

An authorized intake profile SHALL be immutable, identified, versioned, adopted by an authority permitted by Contract 001, and bound to the intake operation before admission outcome determination.

The profile SHALL expose its identity, version, adoption reference, applicable scope, complete component-state mapping, mixed-component rule, successful-state set, failure-requiring-state set, unresolved-state treatment, and replay inputs.

An implementation SHALL NOT select a profile by convenience, ambient configuration, transport, operator preference, or undeclared context.

SRE-001-POLICY-003

The Composite Admission Policy SHALL evaluate component state independently from operation outcome.

Every enumerated component SHALL retain its source identity, category, observed admission state, preservation facts, and any policy mapping result. The policy SHALL then determine exactly one operation-level outcome for the complete enumeration.

SRE-001-POLICY-004

Under the default policy:

- `Accepted` components are compatible with a successful `SourceIntakeRecord`.
- `Redacted` components are compatible with a successful `SourceIntakeRecord` only when constitutional existence, source identity, preservation/integrity facts, redaction state, and authorized redaction basis remain represented.
- `Rejected`, `Unavailable`, `Unsupported`, `Incomplete`, and `Malformed` components require a `SourceIntakeFailureRecord`.
- `Pending` and `Received` are non-terminal observed states; if unresolved at commitment, they require a `SourceIntakeFailureRecord`.

The default policy SHALL use failure if any enumerated component has a failure-requiring or unresolved non-terminal state.

SRE-001-POLICY-005

An authorized versioned profile MAY permit `Unavailable`, `Unsupported`, or `Incomplete` components in a successful `SourceIntakeRecord` only as explicit unresolved admission states and only under the mandatory profile fields, preservation requirements, and prohibitions defined by `SRE-001-SOURCE-009` through `SRE-001-SOURCE-011`.

No profile SHALL permit silent admission of `Rejected` or `Malformed` components, removal of an enumerated component, replacement of an observed state, semantic interpretation, or partial authoritative commitment.

SRE-001-POLICY-006

For identical declared inputs, observed component facts, and bound Composite Admission Policy identity and version, conforming implementations SHALL produce equivalent component-state representations and the same terminal operation outcome.

Component state SHALL remain distinct from operation outcome. A successful `SourceIntakeRecord` containing an explicitly profile-authorized unresolved component state SHALL not be treated as a `SourceIntakeFailureRecord`, and a `SourceIntakeFailureRecord` SHALL not erase the observed component states that caused the failure.

6.4 Source Registration

SRE-001-SOURCE-006

Every admitted source SHALL receive:

exactly one immutable source identity;
one constitutional source category;
one constitutional admission state.

These assignments SHALL occur before successful intake commitment.

Source identities SHALL remain stable throughout the lifetime of request construction.

Identity Assignment

Source identity SHALL identify admitted material only.

It SHALL NOT imply:

canonical request identity;
semantic equivalence;
execution authority;
downstream acceptance.
6.5 Metadata Capture

SRE-001-METADATA-001

Contract 001 SHALL capture only mechanically observable metadata.

Captured metadata MAY include:

observable timestamps;
media type;
declared encoding;
byte length;
supplied filenames;
reference identifiers;
transport-independent structural characteristics.

Semantic interpretation SHALL NOT occur during metadata capture.

Metadata Integrity

Captured metadata SHALL remain traceable to the admitted source.

Later contracts MAY reference metadata.

They SHALL NOT silently alter constitutional metadata.

6.6 Preservation

SRE-001-PRESERVE-002

Immediately following successful admission evaluation, every admitted source SHALL become constitutionally preserved.

Preservation SHALL guarantee recoverable representation throughout subsequent request construction.

Preservation MAY occur through:

immutable content;
immutable references;
cryptographic integrity mechanisms;
implementation-defined preservation strategies.

The implementation mechanism is non-normative.

The preservation guarantee is normative.

6.7 Intake Artifact Construction

SRE-001-ARTIFACT-001

Following successful admission and preservation, Contract 001 SHALL construct exactly one SourceIntakeRecord.

The SourceIntakeRecord SHALL constitute the authoritative representation of admitted source material.

The record SHALL contain sufficient information to support:

downstream interpretation;
provenance;
deterministic reconstruction;
source identification;
submission identification.

The record SHALL preserve every enumerated component and its observed state, including components represented as `Accepted`, `Redacted`, or profile-authorized unresolved states. It SHALL identify the Composite Admission Policy identity and version used for the operation.

Internal representation remains implementation-defined.

6.8 Intake Failure Construction

SRE-001-ARTIFACT-002

If constitutional admission cannot successfully complete, Contract 001 SHALL construct exactly one SourceIntakeFailureRecord.

Failure construction SHALL identify:

submission identity;
observed admission facts;
constitutional failure category;
affected sources when applicable.

The failure record SHALL preserve every enumerated component and its observed state, including components that were individually accepted before the operation-level failure. A failure record SHALL NOT silently remove accepted, rejected, unavailable, unsupported, incomplete, malformed, redacted, pending, or received components from the constitutional account of the intake operation.

Failure artifacts SHALL NOT assign semantic interpretation.

Failure Meaning

Admission failure SHALL indicate only that constitutional intake could not complete.

It SHALL NOT imply:

request invalidity;
user error;
semantic inconsistency;
downstream rejection.
6.9 Atomic Commitment

SRE-001-COMMIT-001

Every intake operation SHALL terminate with exactly one committed constitutional outcome.

Permitted outcomes are:

one SourceIntakeRecord; or
one SourceIntakeFailureRecord.

Both outcomes SHALL NOT exist for the same intake operation.

Neither outcome SHALL be absent.

Commitment Atomicity

Commitment SHALL be atomic.

Partial authoritative admission SHALL NOT exist.

Component-level admission states SHALL NOT be treated as operation-level outcomes. The Composite Admission Policy SHALL determine the one terminal artifact for the complete enumeration before atomic commitment.

Diagnostic information MAY accompany committed artifacts.

Diagnostics SHALL NOT replace constitutional artifacts.

6.10 Downstream Handoff

SRE-001-HANDOFF-001

Successful completion of Contract 001 authorizes only evaluation under Contract 002.

Successful intake SHALL NOT authorize:

semantic interpretation;
request construction;
canonical request identity;
execution;
planning;
provider selection;
generation;
release.

Contract 001 transfers preserved source.

It does not transfer semantic authority.

6.11 Intake Completion

SRE-001-INTAKE-004

An intake operation SHALL be considered complete only after one constitutional outcome has been atomically committed.

Completion SHALL establish:

immutable submission identity;
immutable source identities;
constitutional preservation;
mechanically observable admission facts;
authoritative intake artifact.

Completion SHALL establish nothing further.

Fundamental Intake Lifecycle Invariant

SRE-001-INTAKE-005

Every completed source admission lifecycle SHALL:

preserve every admitted source;
establish immutable submission identity;
establish immutable source identity;
preserve mechanically observable admission facts;
produce exactly one committed intake artifact;

while creating no semantic interpretation, no request meaning, no canonical request identity, no execution authority, and no downstream authorization.
