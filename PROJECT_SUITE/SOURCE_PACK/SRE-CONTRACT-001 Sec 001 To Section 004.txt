1. Purpose

Specification level: Constitutional Concept

SRE-001-PURPOSE-001

This contract establishes the constitutional boundary at which externally supplied or externally referenced material becomes admitted input to the Structured Request Engine.

The purpose of Contract 001 is to define:

what constitutes a source submission;
how source submissions are admitted;
how admitted sources are preserved;
how submission identity and source identity are established;
what mechanically observable information may be recorded during intake;
the authoritative runtime artifacts produced by successful and unsuccessful admission;
the authority limits of source admission.

This contract intentionally does not define:

semantic interpretation;
objective extraction;
constraint identification;
capability inference;
ambiguity analysis;
request normalization;
request validation beyond admission;
canonical request construction;
downstream authorization.

Those responsibilities belong exclusively to subsequent contracts.

Constitutional Objective

SRE-001-PURPOSE-002

The Structured Request Engine SHALL preserve every admitted source as the constitutional ground truth from which all later request construction proceeds.

Interpretation SHALL always occur from admitted source.

Interpretation SHALL never replace admitted source.

Constitutional Outcome

SRE-001-PURPOSE-003

The successful completion of Contract 001 SHALL establish only that supplied material has been admitted into the Structured Request Engine under the rules of this contract.

Successful admission SHALL NOT imply:

semantic correctness;
structural completeness;
interpretation quality;
downstream acceptance;
execution authority;
canonical request construction;
authorization;
feasibility.

Admission records facts.

Admission does not assign meaning.

2. Architectural Identity

Specification level: Constitutional Concept

SRE-001-IDENTITY-001

Contract 001 establishes the Source Admission Boundary of the Structured Request Engine.

It is the exclusive constitutional owner of the transition between externally supplied material and internally admitted source artifacts.

No subsequent contract may redefine the facts established by successful admission.

Source Admission Boundary

SRE-001-IDENTITY-002

The Source Admission Boundary SHALL own only the following transformations:

submission admission;
submission validation for admissibility;
submission identity establishment;
source enumeration;
source identity establishment;
mechanically observable metadata capture;
source preservation;
encoding validation;
creation of authoritative intake artifacts.

No semantic transformation SHALL occur within this boundary.

Observation Without Interpretation

SRE-001-IDENTITY-003

Contract 001 SHALL observe supplied material without assigning semantic meaning.

It SHALL NOT:

interpret natural language;
infer objectives;
infer constraints;
infer capability requirements;
infer assumptions;
reconcile conflicting statements;
normalize semantic representation;
repair malformed meaning;
evaluate request quality.

Any implementation performing such work SHALL do so outside the authority granted by this contract.

Constitutional Position

The Source Admission Boundary occupies the first normative transformation following external submission.

External Submission
        │
        ▼
Source Admission Boundary
        │
        ▼
SourceIntakeRecord
        │
        ▼
Interpretation Boundary
        │
        ▼
Subsequent Contracts

Contract 001 therefore defines facts of admission, not facts of meaning.

3. Authority Boundary

Specification level: Constitutional Concept

SRE-001-AUTH-001

Contract 001 possesses authority only over source admission.

Its authority begins upon receipt of externally supplied material.

Its authority terminates immediately following the successful commitment of either:

one SourceIntakeRecord, or
one SourceIntakeFailureRecord.

No authority established by this contract extends beyond source admission.

Exclusive Responsibilities

SRE-001-AUTH-002

Contract 001 exclusively owns:

submission admission;
admissibility evaluation;
source preservation;
submission identity;
source identity;
intake metadata;
encoding verification;
source categorization;
creation of intake artifacts.

No later contract may silently replace or reinterpret these constitutional facts.

Explicit Prohibitions

SRE-001-AUTH-003

Contract 001 SHALL NOT:

identify objectives;
construct request elements;
identify constraints;
identify capabilities;
classify ambiguity;
reconcile interpretation proposals;
perform normalization;
construct canonical requests;
issue canonical request identities;
authorize execution;
authorize generation;
authorize downstream systems.

Those transformations belong exclusively to subsequent contracts.

Admission Is Not Acceptance

SRE-001-AUTH-004

Admission SHALL mean only that supplied material has satisfied the constitutional requirements for entry into the Structured Request Engine.

Admission SHALL NOT imply:

correctness;
truth;
completeness;
semantic validity;
policy compliance;
structural suitability beyond intake;
authorization;
downstream acceptance.

Admission records the existence of supplied material.

Admission does not endorse it.

Authority Monotonicity

SRE-001-AUTH-005

As artifacts move beyond Contract 001, their semantic precision MAY increase.

Their authority SHALL NOT increase by virtue of admission alone.

The following transformations are constitutionally prohibited:

Submission
      ↓ Admission
Permission

Source
      ↓ Preservation
Authority

Metadata
      ↓ Capture
Execution Rights

Source Identity
      ↓ Assignment
Canonical Request Identity

Admission may preserve.

Admission may identify.

Admission shall never authorize.

Fundamental Authority Invariant

SRE-001-AUTH-006

Every completed source admission operation SHALL preserve:

submission identity;
source identity;
source integrity;
recoverable representation;
mechanically observable admission facts;

while assigning no semantic interpretation, no request meaning, no canonical request identity, and no execution authority.
